• Unlimited habits with custom categories
• Flexible scheduling (daily, weekly, custom)
• Streak tracking with milestone celebrations

• Export your data anytime

**🏆 PERFECT FOR:**
• Fitness enthusiasts building workout routines
• Wellness seekers tracking meditation, sleep, water

• Anyone wanting to improve their life

Unlike other habit trackers, GoalStreak combines beautiful design with powerful features. Our social system keeps you motivated, while detailed analytics help you understand your patterns and improve over time.

Built by habit enthusiasts for habit enthusiasts, GoalStreak is designed to be your long-term companion in personal growth.
