# GoalStreak Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Last Updated:** January 1, 2025

## Introduction

GoalStreak ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- Email address (for account creation)
- Display name (chosen by you)
- Profile information (optional)

### Habit Data
- Habit names and descriptions
- Completion records and dates
- Streak information
- Custom categories and settings

### Social Features
- Friend connections (with consent)
- Shared habit activities (with your permission)
- Social feed interactions

### Technical Data
- Device information
- App usage analytics
- Error logs (for app improvement)

## How We Use Your Information

- Provide and maintain the app service
- Enable social features with friends
- Generate personal analytics and insights
- Improve app performance and features
- Send important notifications about your habits

## Data Sharing

We do NOT sell your personal data. We only share:
- Habit activities with friends (when you choose to share)
- Anonymous usage data for app improvement
- Required legal disclosures (if legally required)

## Data Security

- All data encrypted in transit and at rest
- Secure Firebase backend infrastructure
- Regular security updates and monitoring

## Your Rights

- Access your data anytime
- Delete your account and all data
- Control what you share with friends
- Opt out of analytics (in settings)
