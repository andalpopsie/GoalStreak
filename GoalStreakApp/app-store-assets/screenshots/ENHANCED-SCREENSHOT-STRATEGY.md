### **Caption Design Guidelines**

#### **Format Template**:
```
[EMOTIONAL BENEFIT]
[Feature] • [Feature] • [Feature]
```

#### **Psychological Triggers**:
- **Achievement**: "Build lasting habits", "Track your growth"
- **Social**: "Stay motivated with friends", "Social support"
- **Progress**: "Visual progress tracking", "Detailed insights"
- **Ease**: "Easy setup", "Beautiful design"
- **Personalization**: "Choose from 39+ categories", "Custom icons"

#### **Visual Design Specs**:
- **Main Caption**: Bold, 24-28pt font, high contrast
- **Subcaption**: Regular, 18-20pt font, secondary color
- **Background**: Subtle gradient or solid color for readability
- **Positioning**: Top or bottom third, avoid covering UI elements

## 🎨 **Visual Enhancement Recommendations**

### **Design Elements to Add**
1. **Subtle Drop Shadows**: Make UI elements pop from background
2. **Highlight Callouts**: Circle or arrow pointing to key features
3. **Progress Indicators**: Show "Day 7 streak!" or "85% completion"
4. **Social Proof**: "Join 10,000+ users" or "4.8★ rating"

### **Color Psychology**
- **Green**: Success, growth, positive habits
- **Blue**: Trust, reliability, progress
- **Orange**: Energy, motivation, achievement
- **Purple**: Premium, sophisticated, analytics

### **Typography Guidelines**
- **Primary Font**: Bold, sans-serif (Montserrat Bold or similar)
- **Secondary Font**: Regular weight for subcaptions
- **Contrast**: High contrast for readability on mobile
- **Size**: Large enough to read on small screens
