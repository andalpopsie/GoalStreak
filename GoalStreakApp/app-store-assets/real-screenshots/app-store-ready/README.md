## 📋 App Store Connect Upload Instructions

### Step 1: Access App Store Connect
1. Go to https://appstoreconnect.apple.com
2. Sign in with your Apple Developer account
3. Navigate to "My Apps" → "Goalfer"
4. Click on the version you're preparing (e.g., "1.0")

### Step 2: Upload Screenshots
1. Scroll to "App Store" section
2. Find "iPhone 6.7" Display" section
3. Click "+" to add screenshots
4. Upload all 5 screenshots in order (01, 02, 03, 04, 05)

### Step 3: Use for Other iPhone Sizes
Apple allows you to use 6.7" screenshots for all iPhone sizes:

**For iPhone 6.5" Display:**
- Click "Use 6.7" Display Screenshots"
- Apple will automatically use your 6.7" screenshots

**For iPhone 5.5" Display:**
- Click "Use 6.7" Display Screenshots"
- Apple will automatically use your 6.7" screenshots

This saves time and ensures consistency across all device sizes!

### Step 4: iPad Screenshots (Optional)
