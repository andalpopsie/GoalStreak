# iOS App Store Keyword Optimization Strategy

## Overview
This document outlines the keyword strategy for GoalStreak's iOS App Store listing to maximize discoverability and conversion rates.

1. **habit tracker** - Primary keyword, high search volume
2. **habits** - Core functionality, broad appeal
3. **goals** - Achievement-focused users
4. **productivity** - Professional/personal development market
5. **streaks** - Unique differentiator, gamification appeal

## Keyword Placement Strategy

### App Name
- Primary: "GoalStreak"
- Subtitle: "Social Habit Tracking"

### Keywords Field (100 characters max)
```
habit tracker,habits,goals,productivity,streaks,motivation,social accountability,friends,wellness,fitness
```

### Description Optimization
- **First 170 characters** (visible without "more"): Include primary keywords naturally
- **Opening line**: "Transform your life with GoalStreak - the social habit tracker..."
- **Feature bullets**: Include secondary keywords in feature descriptions
- **Call-to-action**: Include conversion-focused keywords

## A/B Testing Strategy
### Phase 1: Primary Keywords
- Test different combinations of top 5 keywords
- Measure impact on impressions and downloads

### Phase 2: Long-tail Keywords
- Test specific feature-focused keywords
- Measure conversion quality and user retention

### Phase 3: Seasonal Optimization
- Adjust keywords based on seasonal trends
- Test holiday and event-specific terms

## Keyword Density Guidelines
- **App Name**: 1-2 primary keywords
- **Subtitle**: 2-3 secondary keywords
- **Keywords Field**: 8-12 keywords (comma-separated)
- **Description**: Natural integration, avoid keyword stuffing
- **What's New**: Include trending keywords for updates
