# iOS Age Rating and Content Classification

## Age Rating: 4+

### Rationale
Goalfer is designed to be appropriate for all ages, including young children. The app promotes positive behavior change through habit tracking and social accountability, with no objectionable content.

## Content Advisory Questionnaire Responses

### Violence
- **Cartoon or Fantasy Violence**: None
- **Realistic Violence**: None  
- **Prolonged Graphic and Sadistic Realistic Violence**: None

**Justification**: Goalfer contains no violent content of any kind. The app focuses on positive habit formation and social encouragement.

### Sexual Content
- **Sexual Content or Nudity**: None

**Justification**: The app contains no sexual content, nudity, or romantic themes. Profile pictures are user-uploaded but moderated for appropriate content.

### Profanity and Crude Humor
- **Profanity or Crude Humor**: None

**Justification**: The app does not contain profanity or crude humor. All system-generated content is family-friendly. User-generated content (habit names, profile names) is subject to community guidelines.

### Drugs, Alcohol, and Tobacco
- **Alcohol, Tobacco, or Drug Use or References**: None

**Justification**: While users might create habits related to reducing alcohol or tobacco use, the app itself does not promote or reference substance use. Any such habits would be in the context of health improvement.

### Mature/Suggestive Themes
- **Mature/Suggestive Themes**: None

**Justification**: All content is appropriate for children. The app focuses on positive life changes and healthy habits.

### Simulated Gambling
- **Simulated Gambling**: None

**Justification**: The app does not contain gambling mechanics, loot boxes, or random rewards. All achievements and progress are based on actual habit completion.

### Horror/Fear Themes
- **Horror/Fear Themes**: None

**Justification**: The app has a positive, encouraging tone with bright, friendly design elements. No scary or disturbing content.

### Medical/Treatment Information
- **Medical/Treatment Information**: None

**Justification**: While users may track health-related habits, the app does not provide medical advice or treatment information. It's purely for personal tracking and motivation.

### Unrestricted Web Access
- **Unrestricted Web Access**: None

**Justification**: The app does not include web browsing capabilities or links to external websites beyond support and privacy policy.

### Social Networking
- **Social Networking**: Infrequent/Mild

**Justification**: The app includes social features such as:
- Adding friends by email invitation
- Sharing habit progress with friends
- Viewing friends' activity in a feed
- Sending emoji reactions to friends' achievements

These features are designed for positive encouragement and accountability, with strong privacy controls.

### User Generated Content
- **User Generated Content**: Infrequent/Mild

**Justification**: Users can create:
- Custom habit names and descriptions
- Profile names and photos
- Progress sharing with friends

All user-generated content is subject to community guidelines and moderation policies to ensure family-friendly content.

## Privacy and Data Collection

### Data Types Collected
1. **Contact Information**: Email address for account creation and friend invitations
2. **User Content**: Profile photos, habit names, progress data
3. **Usage Data**: App interaction analytics for improvement
4. **Identifiers**: User ID and device ID for functionality

### Data Usage
- **App Functionality**: Core features and user experience
- **Analytics**: App improvement and bug fixing
- **No Advertising**: No data used for advertising purposes
- **No Selling**: User data is never sold to third parties

### Privacy Controls
- Users control what habits are shared with friends
- Profile information sharing is optional
- Data can be deleted upon account deletion
- Comprehensive privacy policy available

## Content Moderation

### Community Guidelines
1. **Respectful Communication**: No harassment or bullying
2. **Appropriate Content**: Family-friendly habit names and profiles
3. **No Spam**: Genuine habit tracking and progress sharing
4. **Privacy Respect**: No sharing of others' personal information

### Moderation Measures
- Automated content filtering for inappropriate terms
- User reporting system for violations
- Manual review of reported content
- Account suspension for repeated violations

## Conclusion

Goalfer is designed to be a safe, positive, and educational app suitable for users of all ages. The 4+ age rating is appropriate given the complete absence of objectionable content and the app's focus on promoting healthy habits and positive social interactions.

The mild social networking and user-generated content features are designed with safety and privacy as top priorities, making the app suitable for family use while providing valuable tools for personal development and goal achievement.
