## A/B Testing Strategy

### Testing Phases
1. **Phase 1**: Test Version A vs Version B (Social vs Results focus)
2. **Phase 2**: Test winning version vs Version C (Problem-Solution)
3. **Phase 3**: Test winning version vs Version D (Feature-Rich)

### Metrics to Track
- **Conversion Rate**: Store listing views to downloads
- **User Quality**: D1, D7, D30 retention rates
- **Engagement**: Habits created per user in first week
- **Social Adoption**: Friend connections in first month
- **Review Sentiment**: App Store rating and review content

### Testing Duration
- Each test: 2-4 weeks minimum
- Seasonal adjustments: Test during different times of year
- Feature updates: Retest when major features are added

### Success Criteria
- **Primary**: Conversion rate improvement >10%
- **Secondary**: D7 retention improvement >5%
- **Tertiary**: Average rating maintenance >4.5 stars

## Localization Considerations

### Key Elements to Localize
1. **Promotional Text**: Adapt cultural motivations
2. **Feature Benefits**: Emphasize locally relevant use cases
3. **Social Proof**: Use region-appropriate testimonials
4. **Call-to-Action**: Culturally appropriate urgency/motivation

### Market-Specific Adaptations
- **US/UK**: Emphasize individual achievement and social connection
- **Germany**: Focus on efficiency and systematic approach
- **Japan**: Highlight group harmony and continuous improvement
- **Brazil**: Emphasize community and celebration of progress

## Seasonal Optimization

### New Year (January)
- Emphasize resolution tracking and fresh starts
- Include "New Year, New You" messaging
- Highlight goal achievement statistics

### Back-to-School (September)
- Focus on routine building and productivity
- Emphasize student-friendly features
- Include academic success stories

### Summer (June-August)
- Highlight fitness and wellness habits
- Emphasize outdoor activities and health goals
- Include summer transformation stories

### Holiday Season (November-December)
- Focus on maintaining habits during busy times
- Emphasize stress management and self-care
- Include gratitude and reflection themes
