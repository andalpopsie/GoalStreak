# How to Test GoalStreak on TestFlight

Thanks for helping test GoalStreak! Here's how to get started:

---

## Step 1: Install TestFlight App

1. Open the **App Store** on your iPhone
2. Search for **"TestFlight"**
3. Download and install the **TestFlight** app (it's free from Apple)

---

## Step 2: Join the Beta

**Option A: If you received an email invite**
1. Open the email from TestFlight
2. Tap **"View in TestFlight"** or **"Start Testing"**
3. This will open the TestFlight app

**Option B: If you received a link**
1. Tap the TestFlight link I sent you
2. It will open the TestFlight app automatically

**Option C: If you received a code**
1. Open the TestFlight app
2. Tap **"Redeem"** in the top right
3. Enter the code I gave you

---

## Step 3: Install GoalStreak

1. In TestFlight, you'll see **GoalStreak** listed
2. Tap **"Install"** or **"Accept"**
3. Wait for the app to download (just like a regular app)
4. Tap **"Open"** to launch GoalStreak

---

## Step 4: Start Testing

1. Go through the onboarding
2. Create an account
3. Start using the app normally
4. Try all the features (habits, social, achievements, etc.)

---

## Step 5: Send Feedback

**Quick Feedback (In TestFlight):**
1. Open the **TestFlight** app
2. Tap **GoalStreak**
3. Tap **"Send Beta Feedback"**
4. Write your thoughts and tap **Send**

**Detailed Feedback (Preferred):**
- Use the feedback template I shared
- Email it to: [your-email@example.com]

**Report Bugs:**
1. When you see a bug, shake your iPhone
2. Take a screenshot
3. Add notes about what happened
4. Send via TestFlight

---

## Important Notes

- **TestFlight apps expire after 90 days** - you'll get updates before then
- **You'll get notifications** when new versions are available
- **Your data is real** - it's saved in Firebase, not test data
- **iOS 13 or later required** - make sure your iPhone is updated

---

## Need Help?

- **Can't install TestFlight?** Make sure you're on iOS 13+
- **Link not working?** Try copying and pasting it in Safari
- **App crashes?** Please report it! That's what testing is for
- **Questions?** Email me at [your-email@example.com]

---

## What to Test

Focus on these areas:
1. **Onboarding** - Is it clear and easy?
2. **Creating habits** - Can you create habits easily?
3. **Daily use** - Mark habits complete, check streaks
4. **Social features** - Add friends, react, comment
5. **Achievements** - Unlock badges
6. **Notifications** - Do reminders work?
7. **Overall experience** - Would you use this daily?
