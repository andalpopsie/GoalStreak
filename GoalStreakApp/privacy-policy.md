## Introduction

GoalStreak ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application GoalStreak (the "App").

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, display name (if provided)
- **Profile Information**: Optional profile picture, user preferences
- **Habit Data**: Habits you create, completion records, notes, and progress
- **Social Data**: Friend connections, friend requests, shared activity updates

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Usage Analytics**: App usage patterns, feature interactions (anonymized)
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system version, app version
- **Performance Data**: Crash reports, error logs (anonymized)

### Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect sensitive personal information
- We do not access your contacts, location, or other apps
- We do not collect information from third-party social media accounts
- We do not share your habit data without your explicit consent

## How We Use Your Information

### Primary Uses
- **App Functionality**: Provide habit tracking, analytics, and progress insights
- **Social Features**: Enable friend connections, friend requests, and activity sharing
- **Account Management**: Create and maintain your user account
- **Data Synchronization**: Sync your data across devices securely

### Secondary Uses
- **App Improvement**: Analyze usage patterns to improve features (anonymized data only)
- **Customer Support**: Respond to your questions and provide assistance
- **Security**: Protect against fraud, abuse, and security threats

## Social Features and Data Sharing

### Friend Connections
- **Friend Requests**: When you send or receive friend requests, we share your display name and email with the other user
- **Activity Sharing**: Friends can see your habit completion activities and progress updates
- **Privacy Control**: You can remove friends and control what information is shared

### Data Visibility
- **Public Information**: Display name and basic profile information visible to friends
- **Private Information**: Specific habit details, notes, and personal data remain private unless explicitly shared
- **User Control**: You have full control over friend connections and can remove friends at any time

## Data Storage and Security

### Firebase Integration
- **Cloud Storage**: Your data is securely stored using Google Firebase services
- **Encryption**: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Access Control**: Strict access controls limit who can view your information
- **Real-time Sync**: Data synchronizes securely across your devices

### Security Measures
- **Authentication**: Secure user authentication and session management
- **Data Protection**: Industry-standard security practices and encryption
- **Regular Updates**: Continuous security monitoring and updates
