# GoalStreak - Product Overview

GoalStreak is a fitness accountability app designed to help users build and maintain healthy habits through tracking, progress insights, and social accountability features.

## Core Purpose
- Help health-conscious individuals, fitness beginners, and people who benefit from social accountability
- Focus on fitness-specific habit tracking with daily, weekly, and monthly progress
- Enable accountability partnerships to monitor and motivate progress
- Provide basic health tracking (weight, sugar-free days)
- Deliver an intuitive, distraction-free mobile experience

## Key Principles
- **Mobile-first**: Thumb-friendly layout optimized for mobile use
- **Minimal clutter**: No feed, no ads in MVP - focus on core functionality
- **Visual motivation**: Color-coded streaks and progress indicators
- **Social accountability**: Friend invites and progress sharing
- **Habit-focused**: Preloaded templates and custom habit creation

## Target Features (MVP)
- Google/Apple Sign-In + Email/Password authentication
- Habit tracking with preloaded templates and custom creation
- Daily checklist, weekly bar charts, monthly streak heatmaps
- Weight logging and sugar-free day counter
- Accountability friend system with privacy controls
- Morning and evening push notifications

## Success Metrics
- Activation: % of users adding at least 1 habit on Day 1
- Retention: D7 & D30 active users
- Engagement: Average habits checked off per user/day
- Accountability Usage: % of users with at least 1 friend
